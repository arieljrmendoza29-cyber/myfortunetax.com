Photos & videos

Invest in a ring light. Good lighting makes a huge difference.
Your phone is powerful. Search TikTok or YouTube for the best camera settings for your phone model.
Keep it clean. Clean background, steady camera, clear video.
Be yourself! People connect with real personalities, not just professional pictures.

What should you post?

Don't just post “I'm doing taxes!” Give people a reason to trust you.

👋Introduce yourself as a Fortune Tax Advisors tax preparer.
📚Share educational tax tips and common mistakes.
😂Create relatable videos about everyday financial situations.
🎯Explain who you help: W-2 employees, self-employed, business owners and more.
🎬Share behind-the-scenes content of your training and tax season prep.
💼Talk about our services and how clients can get started.
🚀

  • Protect client information.

Consistency builds visibility. Visibility builds trust. And trust brings clients! 💰
