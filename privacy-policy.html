Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information Fortune Tax Advisors LLC collects, how we use it, and how we protect it.

Overview

Fortune Tax Advisors LLC (“FTA,” “we,” “us”) provides tax preparation and related services. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have. It applies to our website, forms, scheduling, text messages and the services we provide to clients and job applicants.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details: name, phone number, email and mailing address
  • Tax information: Social Security or ITIN numbers, dates of birth, income documents (W-2s, 1099s), dependents, prior-year returns, and bank details for direct deposit
  • Appointment details, messages, and documents you upload or sign electronically
  • Job applications: contact details, background and anything you include

Information collected automatically

When you visit our website, basic technical information such as device type, browser, pages viewed and approximate location may be collected through cookies and similar tools. See Cookies and tracking.

How we use your information

  • Prepare, review and electronically file your tax return
  • Schedule appointments and respond to your questions
  • Send updates about your return and important tax-season reminders
  • Send marketing messages, only where you have agreed to receive them
  • Review job applications
  • Run, secure and improve our website and services, and meet legal and regulatory requirements

We do not sell your personal information.

Tax return information and IRS Section 7216

As a paid tax return preparer, we are required by federal law, including Internal Revenue Code Section 7216 and its regulations, to protect your tax return information. This includes anything you give us to prepare your return and the return itself.

  • We use your tax return information to prepare and file your return and provide the services you request.
  • We will not use or disclose it for any other purpose, including marketing, unless you give us written consent or the law permits or requires it.
  • Any consent we ask for will be on a separate form that explains what will be used or shared, with whom, and for what purpose. You may decline, and declining will not affect our ability to prepare your return.
  • You may revoke a consent at any time by contacting us in writing.
  • Without your consent, we disclose tax return information only as allowed by law, for example to the IRS or state tax agencies when filing, to team members and service providers who need it to prepare your return, or when required by a court order or other legal process.

How we share information

We share personal information only as needed to provide our services:

  • Tax agencies: the IRS and state agencies, when we file your return
  • Banks and refund processors: if you choose direct deposit or a bank product
  • Service providers: companies that help us operate, such as our tax software, client management and website platform, online forms, scheduling and e-signature providers (see Third-party services). They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • Legal requirements: when required by law, subpoena or court order, or to protect our rights and the safety of others

Text messages (SMS)

By checking the SMS consent box on our forms, you agree to receive text messages from Fortune Tax Advisors, including appointment reminders, updates about your return, alerts, and occasional marketing messages.

  • Consent to receive texts is not a condition of buying any service.
  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to stop receiving messages at any time. You will receive one message confirming you have opted out.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact us at (586) 500-5390.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

We do not share or sell your mobile number or text-messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes. Mobile numbers are shared only with service providers that send messages on our behalf.

Cookies and tracking

Our website and its platform use cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working, remember your preferences, understand how the site is used, and measure the results of our marketing. Some of these tools may be provided by third parties.

  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of the site may not work without them.
  • Our site does not currently respond to “Do Not Track” browser signals.

Third-party services

We use trusted providers to run our business. When you use their tools, their privacy policies also apply:

  • TaxNitro: our website, client management, forms and messaging platform
  • Calendly: appointment scheduling (privacy notice)
  • Jotform: online intake and application forms (privacy policy)
  • Docusign: electronic signatures (privacy notice)
  • Professional tax software: to prepare and e-file returns
  • Instagram: our social media page

How we protect your information

We maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect your information, as required for tax professionals under the FTC Safeguards Rule, including a written information security plan. These include:

  • Access limited to team members who need it to serve you
  • Password-protected accounts and secure systems for sending and storing documents
  • Training our team on privacy, due diligence and client confidentiality

Please send tax documents only through the secure method we provide, not by regular email or text. No online system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you as required by law.

How long we keep information

We keep client records, including copies of tax returns, for as long as required by law and professional standards. Paid preparers must generally keep a copy or list of each return for at least three years, and we may keep records longer when needed to support you or meet legal obligations. When records are no longer needed, we dispose of them securely.

Your choices

  • Ask to see, correct or update your information
  • Ask us to delete information we are not required to keep
  • Opt out of marketing emails with the unsubscribe link, or texts by replying STOP
  • Revoke a Section 7216 consent in writing

To make a request, contact us. We may need to verify your identity first.

Children's privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information online. Information about dependents is collected only from a parent or guardian for tax preparation.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it was last changed.
